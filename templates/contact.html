Frequently Asked Questions

How far in advance should I book?

We recommend booking at least 2–4 weeks in advance to ensure availability. However, we do our best to accommodate last-minute requests when possible!

Do you offer custom packages?

Absolutely! Our packages are a starting point. We love working with clients to create custom setups that match their vision and budget. Contact us for a personalized quote.

What areas do you service?

We're based in Acworth, Georgia and service the greater Atlanta area. Delivery fees may apply depending on distance. Let us know your location!

Do you set up and take down?

Yes! Setup and takedown are included in all our packages. We handle everything so you can focus on enjoying your event.

Can you work with my color scheme or theme?

Of course! We work with your color palette, theme, and preferences. Share your ideas, inspiration photos, or Pinterest boards with us, and we'll bring it to life.

What's your cancellation policy?

Details on our cancellation policy will be discussed during your initial consultation. We're flexible and want to work with you.
